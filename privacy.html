Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Demiurge is a free browser-based simulation. This page explains, plainly, what happens to data when you use it.

The short version

The simulation runs entirely inside your browser. We do not run accounts, we do not ask for personal information, and nothing you do in the simulation is transmitted to us or stored on a server. The only third-party data collection that can occur is advertising, and only if you accept it.

What is stored on your device

  • Your consent choice. When you accept or decline advertising cookies, that single answer is saved in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.
  • Nothing else. Simulation state is held in memory only and is discarded when you close the tab.

Advertising

This site is supported by advertising. If — and only if — you press Accept on the cookie banner, third-party advertising code is loaded. Those partners may then set cookies or similar identifiers on your device in order to display and measure ads, and depending on the partner and your region, to personalise them.

If you press Decline, no advertising code is loaded at all, no advertising cookies are set, and the simulation works exactly the same. Nothing about this site is paywalled or degraded by declining.

Where Google AdSense is used as the advertising partner, Google's own handling of data is described at How Google uses information from sites that use our services. You can manage Google ad personalisation at Google Ads Settings.

Changing your mind

You can withdraw or grant consent at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of the simulation. Clearing your browser's site data for this domain also resets the choice.

Analytics

This site does not currently run any analytics or tracking product. If that changes, this policy will be updated before it is introduced.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data ourselves, there is no account or personal record for us to export or delete. Rights relating to data processed by advertising partners (including access, deletion, and objection under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CPRA) should be directed to those partners, or exercised by declining consent so that no such processing occurs.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project repository.

This document is written to be accurate and readable, but it is not legal advice. If you operate this site commercially at scale, have it reviewed against the rules that apply in your jurisdiction and to your chosen ad network.
